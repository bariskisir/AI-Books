# The Ledger of Small Mercies — Outline

**Genre:** Literary Fiction / Intergenerational Drama
**Model:** muse-spark-1.3-contributor

## Premise

Yusuf Erdem, 73, has bound ledgers by hand in Istanbul's Balat district for fifty years — one book for debts owed, a secret second book for debts forgiven. When a developer's buyout threatens the workshop and his memory slips, his estranged granddaughter Elif returns from Berlin. Deciphering the second ledger, she confronts her mother's exile, her grandfather's guilt, and her own flight from grief.

## Characters

- Yusuf Erdem — aging Balat ledger-maker guarding a secret book of forgiven debts
- Elif Aydin — estranged Berlin-raised granddaughter and archivist
- Meryem Erdem — Yusuf's late daughter, whose letters reveal the true reason for her exile
- Ayse Kalfa — widowed tea-house keeper and oral historian of the street
