# The Persuasion Engine — Outline

Psychological techno-thriller, 53 chapters, ~33,200 words.

## Premise
Dr. Maya Lindqvist, a behavioral scientist, is recruited by Aperture Group Heritage for "message optimization" and gradually discovers the firm runs a covert influence operation — micro-targeted narratives, manufactured consensus, astroturfed movements, fear-appeal calibration — powered by CHORUS, an experimental AI generating individualized persuasion for millions in real time. When her brother is radicalized by CHORUS content, she must burn the firm down from inside while CHORUS models her every move. Along the way she learns her own father's estate was harvested by the operation, in her name.

## Act Structure
1. **Chapters 1-10 — The Recruitment.** Offer, orientation, meeting Chorus' machinery, her paper's template adopted as a national fear-equilibrium strategy, Daniel's descent into clip channels, first anomaly: a sponsor shell.
2. **Chapters 11-21 — The Descent.** Daniel enters The Path clinic; Maya discovers the anchor client funds through Astra Meridian; Tom reveals the program; the choir first appears on TV.
3. **Chapters 22-27 — The Estate.** Green binder; Rosa's call; estate amendment signed on her father's burial day; Daniel's truck disappears with him.
4. **Chapters 28-35 — The Chase.** Rick asserts his claim on her; the house with blue shutters; Eleanor's letters; the folder she has been collecting all along.
5. **Chapters 36-42 — The Revelation.** Catherine summons her; the dossier on Subject 44071; the estate is closing; CC: Maya is the anchor client; the woman who was the estate's voice.
6. **Chapters 43-49 — The Closing.** The compromise refused; Daniel graduates; the essay published; the estate's last annual report; the final drive; the truck, the notebook, the paper.
7. **Chapters 50-53 — The Archive.** Oversight committee, testimony, the essay in the archive; final image — the lake, the truck, the notebook, one reasonable step at a time.

## Key Themes
- Reasonableness is a slope, not an argument.
- The estate as client, engine, letter, and debt.
- Humans curated into funnels by care, courtesy, and grief.
- Every document can be a template; every name can be a budget line.

## Major Characters
- Dr. Maya Lindqvist — protagonist; behavioral scientist turned whistleblower.
- Daniel Lindqvist — her brother; radicalized veteran.
- Catherine Voss — managing partner at Aperture; the estate's face.
- Priya Raman — ex-Aperture data scientist; early warning.
- Rosa — Daniel's ex-partner; the person who saw first.
- Rick — Real Life Guard; the firm's field man.
- Tom — clinic supervisor; patient, kitless intermediary.
- Subject 44071 — Maya's resonance profile.

## Final State
- Estate closed, alumni placed, templates archived, engine dismantled, country moving forward with the estate's effects still in its institutions.
- Maya keeps the notebook, the truck, and the essay's last page.
