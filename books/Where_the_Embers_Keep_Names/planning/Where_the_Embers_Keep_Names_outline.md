# Where the Embers Keep Names — Outline

**Genre:** Horror / Folk Horror
**Model:** muse-spark-1.3-contributor

## Premise

Cartographer Elif Demir arrives at snowbound Karatepe Halt to survey a dying mountain railway and finds a keeper who burns village names to keep the trains running. When she learns her own survey was commissioned to choose the next offering, she must outwit hearth spirits, buried tracks, and her hosts — ending in a burning train ride where she redraws the map in fire.

## Characters

- Elif Demir — disgraced Istanbul cartographer sent to survey Karatepe Halt
- Yusuf Aksoy — elderly station keeper feeding written names to an undying stove
- Meryem Kilic — coal-burner's widow, keeper of the ember atlas
- Doruk Erden — missing rail worker's son who hears the dead rails singing

## Chapters

1. The Last Train to Karatepe — No outgoing train scheduled; the stove must never go out.
2. The Keeper's Stove — Paper slips inscribed with village names feed the fire.
3. Snowbound Ledger — Razored pages; five hamlets missing across forty years.
4. The Coal Widow — The ember atlas: one hamlet offered each winter to spare the line.
5. Singing Rails — Dead rails hum; the survey selects, Elif learns.
6. First Mark — Elif files Çukurhamam as viable; the winter marks her threshold instead.
7. Ocak Night — The hearth rite tries to bind her maps to the atlas.
8. What the Ash Said — The stove refuses certain names; founders' spur map in the ash-bed.
9. The Empty Timetable — The lightless train knocks with Elif's own name.
10. Name for a Name — The law: hamlet, selector, keeper, line, valley — in order.
11. The Missing Hamlet — Çukurhamam erased, unmarked; the winter collects early.
12. The Reversal Ledger — Her finding was theater; selection was made last month.
13. Yusuf's Confession — He traded his assistant twenty years ago; the hearth demands him.
14. Fire Takes Paper — Elif burns her survey; the frost writes her name as price.
15. The White-Out — Elif offers the office, not the officer; Çukurhamam's souls return.
16. Guests at the Halt — The released fill the station; the main line begins to die.
17. The Bargain Room — New terms: memory, not lives — remembrance for release.
18. The Night the Stove Died — Yusuf dies peacefully; Elif becomes the Record.
19. Doruk's Choice — The boy refuses the winter's role and stays.
20. Buried Signal — Founders' bones; the spur is surveyed honestly.
21. The Longest Night — Seven souls confess; the winter accepts truth as price.
22. Lighting the Atlas — The old cloth burned ceremonially; every name honored.
23. The Train That Burns — The memorial engine; Elif rides to the summit with the history-book.
24. Kar Pass Bargain — The new order seared into steel: maintenance, memory, respect.
25. Spring Thaw — Honest trains, a green pass, and the scars on Elif's hands.
