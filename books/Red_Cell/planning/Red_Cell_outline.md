# Red Cell — Outline

**Genre:** Techno-Thriller / Cyber-Thriller
**Status:** Completed — 46 chapters, ~35k words

## Act I — Engagement (Ch. 1–8)
- Ch.1 Contract: Sam Okafor, Helen Brandt, full-scope adversary simulation against Meridian.
- Ch.2 Five Chairs: team assembled — Priya, Dana, Felix, Iris; "RULE 0: WE'RE THE GHOST. THE BUILDING IS THE READER."
- Ch.3–6: OSINT (Priya's organ chart), phishing window, badge cloning, network inventory; Iris spots a second signal.
- Ch.7–8 The Long Game / Second Signal: their noise pattern is being mirrored inside Meridian's logs.

## Act II — The Tenant (Ch. 9–18)
- Ch.9–10 SWALLOW: an APT called Heliotrope is riding the red team's noise; discovery of legacy ghost subnets.
- Ch.11–12 Double Bind / The Body: Helen Brandt found dead; the team is framed as the real attackers.
- Ch.13–15 Framing / A Lawyer's Hand / Safehouse Again: legal defense, Detective Reyes introduced, first decoy canary planted.
- Ch.16–18 The Decoy / The Quiet Strike / Kill the File: counter-operation; Heliotrope feeds them a forged archive tape; first attempt to expose the insider fails.

## Act III — Hunters (Ch. 19–30)
- Ch.19–21 The Second Window / The New Map / The Fifth Floor: team goes back in; decoy floor; ghost subnet traffic suggests an atrium asset.
- Ch.22–23 The Tape / The Elevator: archive tape in the elevator wall; Heliotrope speaks through it.
- Ch.24 The Broken Tape: tape was rewritten; decoy built to make them destroy evidence; insider profile begins.
- Ch.25 The Box: six hours with Reyes; distributor "fox" model emerges.
- Ch.26 The Insider: Renee Okafor, dismissed facilities manager, kept badge; W. Chen second badge; Charlotte money trail.
- Ch.27 Six Hours: second six-hour window with the attorney; distributor profiled.
- Ch.28 Kill the File, Again: news names red team as breach; Heliotrope strikes again.
- Ch.29 The Second Wall: Cristina Mata identified; arrests.
- Ch.30 After Meridian: resolution and the epilogue chapter structure.

## Act IV — Aftermath & Mirror (Ch. 31–46)
- Ramifications: charges dropped, engagement logs exonerate the team; museum tape; second edition of the book; second engagement echoes; colophon-style coda.
- Theme: consent, noise, mirroring — the red cell never stopped being the ghost.
