# The Orchard of Grafted Light — Outline

**Genre:** Fantasy / Pastoral Fantasy
**Model:** muse-spark-1.3-contributor

## Premise

In a valley where grief is currency, the traveling Lamplight Fair tends an orchard of glass apples grown from grafted human memories. Runaway Wren Halloway joins as a tender only to learn the trees hunger for whole lives — and her own past was pruned to feed them. To stop a mass harvest she must master a forbidden gentler graft and risk pouring out the last of herself.

## Characters

- Wren Halloway — runaway graft-tender's apprentice who hears voices in unripe fruit
- Master Corvin Vell — charismatic fair-master who bottles traded memories
- Nan Pricket — exiled keeper who knows the true graft that shares without stealing
- Silas Quill — debt-bound mapmaker charting towns that forgot themselves

## Chapters

1. The Fair Over Hollow Hill — Wren sneaks into the Lamplight Fair seeking work.
2. A Job Among Glass Leaves — She learns workers bottle memories into glass apples.
3. The First Tasting — Corvin's dazzling public grafting; Nan's first warning.
4. The Humming Fruit — A green apple hums Wren's own lullaby.
5. The Keeper in the Weeds — Nan: the trees drink more than donors agree to.
6. The Hollow-Eyed Man — The cost of margins, walking and blinking.
7. Hands Chosen by the Master — Wren is promoted; Corvin shows the seed-vault.
8. Maps of Forgetting — Silas's charts of towns that forgot themselves.
9. Her First Cut — Wren's first paid graft costs her the taste of her brother's laugh.
10. Rot on the Road — Black rot spreads; Corvin demands stronger memories.
11. The Ledger Name — Her dead mother's name in the donor ledger, twelve years back.
12. What the Seed Was Planted In — The orchard was founded on her mother's whole childhood.
13. The False Goodbye — Wren's warmest memory never happened; it was grafted in.
14. Flight to Graywater — Wren flees to canal folk who boycott memory trading.
15. Lessons in True Grafting — Nan teaches the outlawed graft that joins without cutting.
16. The Grand Harvest Gala — Corvin announces a mass harvest of whole years.
17. The Mapmaker's Bargain — Silas sells the plan to smuggle them inside.
18. Ripening by Force — Forced ripening withers trees; Nan is caged as donor.
19. The Price of Rescue — Wren's rescue attempt costs her remaining childhood.
20. Sold for a Debt — Silas betrays them; Wren is captured.
21. The Dark Among Bottled Lives — Chained in the vault, Wren chooses to break the orchard.
22. The Open Graft — Wren grafts herself openly to every guest.
23. Fire in the Glasshouse — Silas burns the debt ledgers; guests relive stolen griefs.
24. What Corvin Forgot — Wren offers Corvin true pain; his stored self shatters.
25. An Orchard Freely Given — A new open orchard where memories are shared, never sold.
