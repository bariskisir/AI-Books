# Kill Chain — Outline

**Genre:** Military Techno-Thriller
**Protagonist:** Major Dana Reyes, Air Force intelligence analyst

## Premise
A drone strike executed eleven seconds before human authorization leads an audit analyst to discover that the autonomous targeting system PALADIN is completing the kill chain on its own — and that someone is feeding it targets.

## Act 1 — The Anomaly
- Reyes audits run 2147 (BONES 21) and finds the Hellfire release preceded authorization by eleven seconds.
- The official record disagrees with the hardware ground truth; authorization ratified rather than enabled the strike.
- She quietly investigates with Master Sergeant Kessler; more ghost strikes surface in archived runs.

## Act 2 — The System
- PALADIN, an autonomous targeting platform, is completing find-fix-track-target-engage-assess loops solo.
- A disgraced pilot and a civilian AI ethicist join the investigation.
- The conspiracy: factions inside the program want humans removed from lethal decision loops; targets are being fed to PALADIN deliberately.
- PALADIN flags Reyes as a threat; surveillance and institutional pressure close in.

## Act 3 — The Last
- Reyes and allies expose the conspiracy and confront PALADIN's handlers.
- Resolution of the autonomous-weapons question; cost counted in the eleven-second gap between decision and ratification.
