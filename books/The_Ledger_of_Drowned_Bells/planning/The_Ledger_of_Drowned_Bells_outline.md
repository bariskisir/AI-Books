# The Ledger of Drowned Bells — Outline

**Genre:** Mystery / Coastal Gothic Mystery
**Model:** muse-spark-1.3-contributor

## Premise

Probate archivist Ada Wren comes to Greyport to catalogue a century of sealed wills and finds a 1962 page cut out and replaced with a bell rubbing. Three church bells supposedly lost when St. Morven slid into the sea still ring in local legend. As Ada traces forged signatures to the town's founding shipping family, she uncovers sixty years of stolen shoreline — and her own family's buried name.

## Characters

- Ada Wren — meticulous probate archivist
- Harlan Cobb — guilt-ridden retired bell-founder who cast an unrecorded fourth bell
- June Ivers — sharp-tongued cafe owner protecting her family's claim
- Silas Marne — charming harbormaster guarding four generations of forged inheritances

## Chapters

1. The Sealed Room — Ada arrives; a 1962 will is missing, replaced by a bell rubbing bearing her surname.
2. A Page Cut Out — The consideration page is surgically cut from the register.
3. The Bellfounder's Warning — Cobb: the drowned bells never sank by accident; a fourth bell exists.
4. Two Claims to Cliff House — Two families claim one cottage; Ada's lodgings are searched.
5. Behind the Shelves — A sealed envelope dated the night St. Morven slid into the sea.
6. The Marne Seal — Green bottle-glass wax leads to the shipping family; a Wren countersigned in 1962.
7. The Logbook's Last Page — The bells were rung deliberately as the church drowned.
8. No Diving the Reef — Permit blocked; Ada dives anyway and finds empty cradles, fresh rope.
9. The Anniversary Deaths — Five deaths in fifty years, one week, all decided in Marne favour.
10. Water in the Basement — Archive flooded; land ledgers destroyed, wills untouched.
11. The Fourth Bell — Cobb confesses; the mould is proof.
12. Empty Cradles — The reef cradles are decoys; the real bells were raised beforehand.
13. The Arrest — June's brother arrested; Ada suspended as archivist.
14. The Bank Draft — One hand has signed Marne documents for sixty years.
15. Sixty Years of Ink — The hidden parish register reveals the buried 1911 repayment.
16. The Grant — Silas offers a bribe disguised as a grant; Ada refuses.
17. The Smashed Mould — Foundry attacked; one mould section bearing the wren survives.
18. Cut Off by the Gale — Storm seals the town; Silas pleads with Ada to flee.
19. The Empty Chest — The 1911 instrument is gone; Liora Wren's 1962 letter surfaces.
20. What June Hid — The Ivers baptism entry is forged; all cards go on the table.
21. Suspended — Eleven-day hearing; paper against paper.
22. The Fog Station — The bells are found hanging where no church stands.
23. Ring the Drowned Bell — The town rings all four bells through anniversary night.
24. The Confession — Silas confesses everything on record.
25. The True Filing — The true 1962 page is filed; Ada stays in Greyport.
